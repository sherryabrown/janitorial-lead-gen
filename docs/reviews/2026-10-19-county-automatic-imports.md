## Behavior and safeguards

Confirmed Admin county collection now saves valid results automatically through `county-automatic-v1`. It reuses current interpretation receipts, literal primary and signed supporting evidence, confirmed county/city scope, dates, routine service validation, canonical identity planning, native SQL rehearsal, exact package binding, guarded transactions and verified readback. No candidate selection, written reason or approval hash is required for this county policy.

Source plus stable record identity determines the canonical lead. A current observation can supersede an unimported pending parent. A later sibling can reuse that canonical lead only when a processed observation with the same source, stable record and pending parent already has the exact canonical link. Pending ancestors remain untouched. Missing/conflicting links, ignored observations, older evidence and unsupported classification changes remain exceptions. Validation revision 2 permits continuation of the identified earlier rejection without another search; only verified persistence of that exact intake clears its old exception.

Notes, sales stages and owner-managed fields remain protected. Source facts have a narrow update allowlist. Later discovery waves have distinct durable keys, and uncertain writes require reconciliation rather than resend. Normal interpretation waits now describe saved results/source gaps; Admin grids and long actions fit narrow screens.

SAM automatic imports, independent state and standalone reviewed workflows, source-only behavior, queues, Companies, email previews, AI settings and budgets remain unchanged. No migration, new table, paid provider or new import infrastructure was needed. Historical requests belonging to deleted verification actors were not reactivated, reassigned or bulk-imported.
